## 2. Node sets

- **Design.** The E3 Tier A design: border sets across one of the 4 most strongly connected community pairs, and within-module sets. Sizes are 7–12, with 4 slots per kind and size.
- **Adaptation for sparse networks.**
    - **Why.** In dense brain connectomes a uniform random subset is connected. In these networks most are not, which leaves models that are undefined (no internal edges) or split into independent parts. This was found while building the generator, before any data existed.
    - **Change.** Every set is therefore grown as a connected *snowball*: start at a seed node, then repeatedly add a uniformly chosen node of the pool that is adjacent to the current set.
    - **Border sets:** the pool is the union of the pair's two communities, and the seed is one of the max(2n, n + 2) most ambiguous nodes (as in E3). A set is accepted only with at least 2 nodes from each community.
    - **Within-module sets:** the pool is one module with at least n nodes.
- **Randomness.** One random stream (seed 20261129), with up to 50 attempts per slot. A slot is skipped, and recorded, when no attempt gives a new accepted set.
- **Result:** 236 sets (120 border, 116 within).
    - Karate is missing 4 within-module sets at n = 11–12, because its modules are too small for new connected sets.
    - Sets per size, for n = 7–12: 40, 40, 40, 40, 39 and 37.
- **Model.** Each set is analysed as its own field-free network (own modularity matrix, h = 0), with β = β̃/σ, β̃ = 1 primary and β̃ = 2 secondary. This is the E3 model.
- **Code:** `experiments/e6_instances.py`. It is deterministic; this is checked by regenerating the sets twice.

## 4. Quantities (exact, partition space)

- **Code.** `qsr2/exact_ps.compute_ps`, exactly as in E3c. It computes partition-space gaps and exact asymptotic variances at β̃ = 1 and 2.
- **Chains:** local, label-swap, uniform, the untuned quench (the quantum proposal), QAOA-MC at angle 0, Swendsen–Wang and Wolff, plus heat-bath at its per-cost gap.
- **r = log₂(gap_quantum / gap_oracle).** The oracle is the best classical chain for each set. This is the E3 endpoint-A definition, with PT excluded.
- **Steps to accuracy:** log₂(s²_best classical / s²_quantum).

## 5. Primary endpoints

- **E6-A, the rule transfers.**
    - **Test:** among sealed predicted wins versus the rest, the share of sets with r > 0. One-sided Fisher exact test over all 236 sets.
    - **Passes iff** p < 0.05.
- **E6-B, the advantage transfers.**
    - **Test:** the mean r over the predicted-win class must exceed 0, with its 95 % CI excluding 0. The CI is a stratified bootstrap within (network, size) cells, with 10,000 resamples and seed 53.
    - **Inference** is conditional on these five networks.
- **E6 passes** iff E6-A and E6-B both pass.
- **E6-C, prospective test of the projection procedure.**
    - **The procedure** (`qsr2/projection.py`, frozen with this protocol) fits y = a + b·n to set-level values by least squares. Because y is a log₂ ratio, this is an exponential law with b bits gained per added node.
    - **Prediction:** the mean of the new sets at a larger size, with a 95 % interval from a stratified bootstrap. The interval covers both the uncertainty of the fitted line and the spread of new sets.
    - **Validation** (`tests/test_projection.py`): exact on noise-free lines, and close to nominal coverage on simulated data.
    - **Sealed prediction:** the procedure is fitted to all sets at n = 7–10 (metric r). It predicts the mean r of the 39 sets at n = 11 and of the 37 at n = 12, and `experiments/e6_project.py` seals these predictions before the test stage.
    - **Guards in the code:** the test stage refuses to start until the sealed projection exists, and the projection refuses to be written once any test-size row exists. `tests/test_e6.py` checks both.
    - **Passes iff** the observed mean r at n = 11 and at n = 12 both lie inside their sealed 95 % intervals.

## 8. Outcomes (fixed in advance)

| E6 result | Statement allowed in the paper |
|---|---|
| E6-A and E6-B pass | The routing rule and the quantum advantage carry over to non-brain networks (social, co-occurrence and synthetic benchmarks). The claim becomes "frustrated network sub-structures", not only brain networks |
